      HOW TO PLAY

      Your mind is your superpower.

      1. Choose a warrior. Each has different health, attack and a unique skill.
      2. Questions appear automatically. Answer correctly to attack.
      3. Three correct regular answers fill your charge. Your skill activates automatically for the next three questions. Correct answers use the skill; wrong answers still use one of those three questions.
      4. After your move, the guardian hits back unless you defeated it. A wrong answer skips your move and shows an explanation.
      5. Clear all five stages to earn the crown. Health refills and skill charge starts at zero at each new stage. You can retry a lost stage.

      No timer. No rush. Every question is a chance to learn.
